## Summary

Implement time-blocking as a core productivity strategy to enable sustained deep work sessions.

## Details

**What is Time Blocking:**
- Divide your day into dedicated blocks for specific activities
- Each block has a single purpose - no multitasking during the block
- Include buffer periods between blocks for transitions and interruptions

**My Current System:**
```
07:00 - 08:00: Morning routine & planning
08:00 - 10:00: Deep work block #1 (most important task)
10:00 - 10:30: Buffer/break
10:30 - 12:00: Deep work block #2 or meetings
12:00 - 13:00: Lunch break
13:00 - 14:00: Administrative tasks, email
14:00 - 16:00: Deep work block #3 or collaborative work
16:00 - 17:00: Wrap-up, planning for tomorrow
```

### Key Points

1. **Buffer periods are essential** - Without them, any disruption cascades through your day
2. **Match tasks to energy levels** - Put demanding cognitive work during peak hours
3. **Protect deep work blocks fiercely** - These should be treated as unbreakable meetings with yourself

## Takeaways

Time-blocking works best when paired with buffer periods for interruptions. The key insight: productivity isn't about filling every minute, it's about protecting focused time for high-value work.

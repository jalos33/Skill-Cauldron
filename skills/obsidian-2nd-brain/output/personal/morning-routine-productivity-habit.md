## Summary

Optimized morning routine significantly improved daily productivity and mental clarity.

## Details

**Current Morning Routine:**

1. **6:30 AM** - Wake up, no snooze
2. **6:35 AM** - Hydrate (large glass of water)
3. **6:40 AM** - 10-minute light stretch/yoga
4. **7:00 AM** - Shower
5. **7:20 AM** - Breakfast + coffee while reviewing daily plan
6. **7:45 AM** - Leave for work

**What Changed Last Month:**
- Previously checked email/phone first thing (caused reactive day)
- Now complete routine before any digital input
- Added consistent wake time (was previously variable by hours)

### Key Points

1. **No screens until after breakfast** - Huge difference in mental state
2. **Exercise even if brief** - 5 minutes better than none, 10 is ideal
3. **Review plan before starting work** - Ensures day begins with intention

## Takeaways

A consistent morning routine sets the tone for an entire day's productivity. The key insight: what you do first thing determines your default state. Starting intentionally (not reactively) creates a productivity head start.

## Summary

Implemented daily self-reflection alongside team standups to improve personal productivity awareness.

## Details

**New Standup Format:**

Before sharing in team meeting, I now complete a 2-minute quick reflection:
1. What was my most important accomplishment yesterday?
2. What am I committed to delivering today?
3. Any blockers requiring attention?

**Benefits Observed:**

- **Increased accountability**: Writing it down first makes commitment more concrete
- **Better focus during standup**: Arrive prepared, reduces rambling
- **Self-awareness tracking**: Weekly review of reflections shows patterns in productivity
- **Blocker awareness**: Identifying blockers earlier prevents delay cascades

### Key Points

1. **Write before verbal** - The act of reflection clarifies thinking
2. **Consistency matters more than length** - 2 minutes daily beats 30 minutes weekly
3. **Track blockers systematically** - Patterns emerge showing systemic vs random issues

## Takeaways

Daily productivity reflection during standups creates both team transparency and personal accountability. The key insight: the preparation for standup is as valuable as the standup itself.
